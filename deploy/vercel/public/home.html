Image to ink

From photo to tablet

  1. Drop in a photo

    A photo of your notes, a sketch, a logo or a PDF. On a phone you can take the photo right there.

  2. See the ink

    Pick your tablet and a style. The preview shows exactly what you will get.

  3. Put it on the tablet

    Send it straight to reMarkable, or download the notebook and copy it over.

rmink in your own Claude

Sketches, drawings and workbooks run in your own Claude, on your own Claude account. Add rmink as an MCP server in Claude Code or Claude Desktop and ask.

claude mcp add rmink -- uvx --from 'rmink[mcp]' rmink mcp

You also need rmapi to reach your tablet, and you run a few commands in a terminal.

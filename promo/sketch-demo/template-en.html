Scenarios

Eight ways to use it

The demo at the top shows scenarios 1, 3 and 8, with a legend from scenario 2. Scenarios 4 to 7 have their own animations above. All of them build on the same model: the drawing is kept as data between rounds, so every revision knows what the previous one held.

Colour and greyscale

  • Paper Pro and Move: every category gets its own colour. Electrical is red, plumbing blue, changes green and comments magenta.
  • rm2 and Paper Pure: the same categories are told apart by solid, dashed, dotted or dash-dot lines and by width.
  • Switch tablets in the demo to see the difference.

Who does what

  • Claude reads handwriting and decides what each group is: a wall, a measurement, a symbol or a remark.
  • rmink handles the geometry. It fits lines and circles, squares up corners, checks the measurements and draws.
  • The interpretation runs in the user's own Claude, so rmink stays free.

To verify on a tablet

  • Does the tablet keep rmink's stroke ids after a sync? That is what lets only the new strokes be picked up.
  • Can you select the Kommentarer (comments) layer and draw on it?
  • How well are real handwritten measurements such as 45° and Ø110 read?
rmink is free and open source. The sketch, the marks, the variants, the detail and the photo in the demo are synthetic, as are the PDF sheet and the DXF file in the export demo. The groups, the measurement check, the clean drawings and which strokes count as new come from rmink 2.2.1 with the phase 1 code.

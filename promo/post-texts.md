## Tips

- **Write a short personal intro** ("I built this because…"). Groups react better to a person than to an ad.
- **Post the video natively** (upload the MP4), not as a YouTube link. Native video autoplays in the feed.
- **Answer comments during the first hour.** It keeps the post visible.
- **A Pro or Move owner who tries it is gold.** Ask them for a photo of the result on their tablet. It also verifies the beta devices.
- **Don't post the same text in many groups on the same day.** Facebook may flag it as spam. Spread it out over a week.

## English

I write a lot by hand, but my notes always ended up as flat pictures on my reMarkable. So I built a tool that turns them into real ink. ✍️

**rmink** turns a photo of notes, a sketch, a portrait or a PDF into native reMarkable pen strokes. Not a picture behind your text: strokes you can erase, lasso, move, resize and draw on, just like your own handwriting.

How it works:
📸 Drop a photo on rmink.vercel.app
👀 See the ink instantly and pick your tablet
⬇️ Download the notebook
🔌 Copy it over USB or drag it into the reMarkable app

It also works with Claude: add rmink as an MCP server and ask Claude to turn a photo into a notebook.

It's free and open source. Tested on reMarkable 2. Paper Pro, Move and Pure are in beta, so I'd love to hear how it works on yours!

🎬 Watch the demo (100 s): https://rmink.vercel.app/video
💻 Source: https://github.com/thagvall/rmink
